I build pricing and risk models by day, and write about markets, code, and careers on the side.

I'm Jehoiakim — a quant working in fixed income and credit markets, originally trained in mathematics and computer science in France. This site is where I share what I'm learning, take on the occasional consulting project, and keep a public record of the things I build.

What I do

Quantitative strategy

My day job: pricing, risk, and portfolio optimisation for credit and fixed income desks — from index quoting models to structured financing analytics.

Consulting, on the side

I take on a small number of independent projects in data-driven forecasting and automation — helping businesses replace guesswork and manual process with proper models. Get in touch if that sounds useful to you.

Writing & teaching

I write about quantitative finance, career transitions into the field, and the odd bilingual (French/English) perspective. I also founded Studies and Careers, a YouTube project helping students navigate academic and professional choices.
